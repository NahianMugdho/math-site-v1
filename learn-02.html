২. Concept 2: Basic Operations, Factors, Multiples

  • Adding (যোগ করা) numbers gives the sum or total (যোগফল). 2 + 3 = 5, so the sum of 2 and 3 is 5.

  • Subtracting/deducting (বিয়োগ করা) one number from another gives the difference (বিয়োগফল). 5 − 3 = 2, so the difference of 5 and 3 is 2.

  • Multiplying (গুণ করা) numbers gives the product (গুণফল). 2 × 3 = 6, so the product of 2 and 3 is 6.

    • 2 and 3 are factors (উৎপাদক) of 6.

    • 6 is a multiple (গুণিতক) of both 2 and 3.

    • 6 is divisible (বিভাজ্য) by both 2 and 3.

    • Each number is divisible by its factors, so factors are also called divisors (গুণনীয়ক).

  • Dividing (ভাগ করা) one number by another gives the quotient (ভাগফল). 6 / 3 = 2.

  • Dividing one integer by another yields either a zero remainder (ভাগশেষ) or a positive-integer remainder. 6 is evenly divided (নিঃশেষে বিভাজ্য) by 3 and the remainder is 0. But 7 / 2 = 3 plus a remainder of 1.

  • An integer N is divisible by an integer x when N divided by x yields a zero remainder.

  • If N is divisible by x, then N is also divisible by all factors of x. 32 is divisible by 16, so 32 must be divisible by 1, 2, 4 and 8, which are all factors of 16.

  • If the remainder is r when p is divided by k, then p = kq + r, where q is an integer.
