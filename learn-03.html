৩. Concept 3: Divisibility Rules

  • By 2: the last (units, একক স্থানীয়) digit is 2, 4, 6, 8 or 0. Numbers divisible by 2 are even numbers.

  • By 3: the sum of its digits is divisible by 3.

  • By 4: the number formed by the last two digits is divisible by 4.

  • By 5: the last digit is 0 or 5.

  • By 6: divisible by both 2 and 3. Simply, if an even number is divisible by 3, it is divisible by 6.

  • By 8: the number formed by the last three digits is divisible by 8.

  • By 9: the sum of its digits is divisible by 9.

  • By 10: the last digit is 0.

  • By 11: the sums of the digits in the odd positions (from the right) and in the even positions are equal or differ by a multiple of 11.

    • Example: 29623. From the right, 3 is the first digit, 2 the second, 6 the third, 9 the fourth, 2 the fifth.

    • Odd positions (1st, 3rd, 5th): 3 + 6 + 2 = 11. Even positions (2nd, 4th): 2 + 9 = 11.

    • Difference = 11 − 11 = 0, so the number is divisible by 11.
