৫. Concept 5: HCF and LCM

  • Factors of 12: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. Factors of 16: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16.

  • Common factors of 12 and 16: 1, 2, 4. The highest is 4.

  • So the HCF (highest common factor, গসাগু বা গরিষ্ঠ সাধারণ গুণনীয়ক) of 12 and 16 is 4.

  • Multiples of 12: 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, 84, 96, …, 144, …, 192, …

  • Multiples of 16: 16, 32, 48, 64, 80, 96, 112, …, 144, …, 192, …

  • Common multiples: 48, 96, 144, 192, … The least is 48.

  • So the LCM (lowest common multiple, লসাগু বা লঘিষ্ঠ সাধারণ গুণিতক) of 12 and 16 is 48.

  • Product rule: the product of the HCF and LCM of two numbers equals the product of those numbers. If the HCF of a and b is x and the LCM is y, then ab = xy.

    • HCF of 4 and 5 is 1; LCM is 20. So 4 × 5 = 20 × 1 = 20.
