৬. Concepts 6, 7: Fractions, Decimals

Adding and subtracting fractions — example: 5 1/3 + 1/4 − 4/3

  1. First convert improper/mixed fractions: 5 1/3 = (5×3 + 1)/3 = 16/3. So 16/3 + 1/4 − 4/3.

  2. Find the LCM of the denominators (12), divide it by each denominator and multiply each numerator by the answer: (4×16 + 3×1 − 4×4)/12 = (64 + 3 − 16)/12 = 51/12.

Multiplying and dividing fractions — example: 5 1/3 × 3/8 ÷ 1/4

= 16/3 × 3/8 × 4/1 = 8.

Dividing by 1/4 is the same as multiplying by 4. Reverse the fraction after the division sign and change the sign to multiplication.

Raising and reducing fractions

  • Multiplying both numerator and denominator by the same number doesn't change the value: 25/100 = (25×4)/(100×4) = 100/400.

  • Dividing both by the same number doesn't change the value: 25/100 = 1/4. Dividing by the HCF of numerator and denominator gives the fraction in its lowest form.

Adding and subtracting decimals — example: 3.09 + 3.009 − 3.9 + 39

Align the decimal points and add/subtract as normal. Adding zeros after a decimal doesn't change its value: 3.090 + 3.009 − 3.900 + 39.000 = 41.199.

Multiplying decimals by a power of 10: 3.1614 × 100 = 316.14. The point moves one place right for every zero after 1; since 100 has two zeros, it moves 2 places.

Dividing decimals by a power of 10: 316.14 ÷ 100 = 3.1614. The point moves one place left for every zero after 1; 100 has two zeros, so 2 places.

Note: the source page states these two directions the other way round in its side-notes; the worked results above (×100 makes the number larger, ÷100 makes it smaller) are the correct ones.

Multiplying decimals: 2.5 × .008. First multiply as if there were no decimals: 25 × 8 = 200. In 2.5 the point is 1 place from the right; in .008 it is 3 places from the right. So put the point 1 + 3 = 4 places from the right: 2.5 × .008 = 0.0200.

Dividing decimals: eliminate the decimals by multiplying numerator and denominator by the same power of 10, then divide normally.

  • 26.5 ÷ 5 = (26.5×10)/(5×10) = 265/50 = 5.3

  • 26.5 ÷ 0.05 = (26.5×100)/(0.05×100) = 2650/5 = 530

Two fractions are equivalent if multiplying or dividing both the numerator and denominator of the first by the same number gives the second. E.g. 1/2 and 4/8 are equivalent.
