৯. Concept 11: Average

  • The average (গড়) of a group of numbers represents a central value for them.

  • Average = Sum of elements ÷ Number of elements. E.g. the average of 6, 9, 18 and 15 is (6 + 9 + 18 + 15)/4 = 12.

  • If all numbers in a set are the same, that number is the average.

  • If the numbers are not all the same, the average is greater than the smallest and less than the largest number; at least one number is below the average and at least one is above.

  • When n numbers form an arithmetic sequence:

    1. If n is odd, the average is the middle term.

    2. If n is even, the average is the average of the two middle terms.

  • Weighted average: multiply each number by its weight, add the products and divide by the total weight (total number of numbers in the set).

  • Sum = average × number of elements. E.g. the average of 10, 15, 20 is 15, so Sum = 15 × 3 = 45 = 10 + 15 + 20.
