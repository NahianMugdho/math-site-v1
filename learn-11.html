১১. Concept 14: Percent

  • Percent (শতকরা) means per hundred, or number out of 100. It can be written as a fraction with denominator 100 or as a decimal: 45% = 45/100 = 0.45.

  • To find a percent of a number, multiply the number by the percent expressed as a decimal or fraction: 20% of 90 = (20/100) × 90 = 18, or 0.2 × 90 = 18.

  • Percents greater than 100% are numbers greater than 1: 300% = 300/100 = 3.

  • Fraction → percent: multiply by 100. 3/4 = 3/4 × 100% = 75%; 1/4 = 25%.

  • Decimal → percent: convert to a fraction, then multiply by 100%. 0.1 = 1/10 = 10%; 0.54 = 54/100 = 54%.

  • Percent increase (or decrease): find the amount of the increase (decrease), divide it by the original amount, and express the quotient as a percent.

    • If a price increases from $24 to $30, the increase is 30 − 24 = 6. Percent increase = 6/24 = 0.25 = 25%.
