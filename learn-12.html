১২. Concept 15: Profit & Loss

  • Profit = Selling Price − Cost. Loss = Cost − Selling Price.

  • Profit or loss is always calculated on the cost.

    • Sold at 20% profit → sold at 120% of cost.

    • Sold at 20% loss → sold at 80% of cost.

  • Marking up/down means raising/lowering the selling price; it is always calculated on the (previous) selling price.

    • Marked up by 20% → new price is 120% of the previous selling price.

    • Marked down by 20% → new price is 80% of the previous selling price.

  • 100% of cost is the cost itself. Keeping this in mind makes problems easier.

    • Example: a book was sold at 20% profit and the profit was 40 tk. So 20% of cost = 40, 100% of cost = 200. The cost of the book was 200.

  • Discount is calculated on the selling price. If an item is sold at 20% discount, it is sold at 80% of the previous selling price.
