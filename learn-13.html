১৩. Concept 16: Interest

  • Principal is the money deposited or borrowed.

  • Interest is the extra money paid as the cost of the principal, expressed as a percentage. It generally means a yearly rate: a 5% rate means 100 Tk principal earns 5 Tk interest every year.

  • Total Amount = Principal + Interest Amount.

  • Simple interest: the principal remains unchanged. At 5%, 100 Tk earns 5 Tk every year.

    • Formula: I = PNR, where I = interest, P = principal, N = time, R = yearly interest rate.

  • Compound interest: the interest earned becomes part of the principal, and the rate applies to principal + interest.

    • Formula: I = P(1 + r)ⁿ, where I = total amount (principal + interest), P = principal, N = time (n), R = yearly interest rate (r).
