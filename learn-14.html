১৪. Concept 17: Inequalities

  • An equation has an equal sign (=) between two mathematical statements. Inequalities have a greater-than (>) or less-than (<) sign between statements. Inequalities are not equations.

  • There are also "greater than or equal" (≥) and "less than or equal" (≤) signs.

    • x > 5 means x is greater than 5. x can be 6, 7, 8, 9, … ∞ (x cannot be 5).

    • x ≥ 5 means x is greater than or equal to 5. x can be 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, … ∞ (x can be 5).
