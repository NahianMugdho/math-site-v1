১৫. Concept 18: Ratio & Proportion

  • A ratio is simply an expression of a fraction.

    • The ratio of a to b is written a : b ("a is to b") and means a divided by b (a/b).

    • The ratio of b to a is written b : a and means b/a.

    • These two ratios are inverses of one another, since a/b and b/a are reciprocals.

  • If a set of objects is divided into 2 groups in the ratio a : b, the first group contains a/(a+b) of the objects and the second group contains b/(a+b). A similar rule applies to groups of 3 or more.

  • If two numbers are in the ratio a : b, then for some number x, the first number is ax and the second is bx.

  • The equality of two ratios is called a proportion. a : b = 3 : 4 or a/b = 3/4 is a proportion.
