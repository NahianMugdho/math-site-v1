১৭. Concept 20: Work Problems

  • We generally use the unitary method (ঐকিক নিয়ম) to solve these problems.

  • If A can complete a work in n days, A does 1/n of the work in 1 day. Alternatively, if A does 1/n of the work per day, A will complete the work in n days.

    • Example: if A can do a work in 5 days, in 1 day he does 1/5 of the work. If A does 1/5 of the work daily, he will complete the work in 5 days.

  • When more people do the same job, the number of days/hours required for the job declines.
