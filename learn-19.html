১৯. Concept 22: Mixture Problems

  • Important formula: C = (C₁Q₁ + C₂Q₂) / (Q₁ + Q₂)

    • C₁ = concentration of the first mixture

    • Q₁ = quantity of the first mixture

    • C₂ = concentration of the second mixture

    • Q₂ = quantity of the second mixture
