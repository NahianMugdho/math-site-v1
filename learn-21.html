২১. Probability

  • Probability is the extent to which an event is likely to occur. It is measured by the ratio of the favorable cases to the whole number of cases possible, and is a number between 0 and 1 (0 = impossible, 1 = certain). The higher the probability, the more certain the event.

  • Probability of an event = Desired events / Total events

  • Example: a die is rolled; find the probability of an even number. Total sides = 6 {1,2,3,4,5,6}; even numbers = 3 {2,4,6}. Probability = 3/6 = 1/2.

Probability of an event occurring at least once

First find the probability of the event not occurring at all, then subtract it from 1. (1 is the probability of everything happening; removing the unwanted case leaves every other possibility.)

  • Example: flip a fair coin three times; find the probability of at least one head. Probability of three tails = 1/2 × 1/2 × 1/2 = 1/8. So P(at least one head) = 1 − 1/8 = 7/8.
