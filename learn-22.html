২২. Chapter 1: Angles, Triangles

In the admission test, basic geometric concepts are tested. If you know basic geometric terms, figures, some theorems and shortcut methods, problems can be solved in less than the allocated time.

Angles

  • An angle's meeting point is the vertex and its two arms are legs. The ∠ sign indicates an angle.

    • A circle holds 360° at its centre.

    • When two lines intersect, the four angles formed sum to 360°.

    • All angles around a point sum to 360°.

  • Right angle (সমকোণ): a 90° angle, marked with a small box at the vertex.

    • When two lines meet at 90° they are perpendicular to each other, shown with the ⊥ sign.

    • An angle that merely looks like 90° cannot be assumed to be a right angle — the problem must mention ⊥ or 90° / right angle.

  • Acute angle (সূক্ষ্ম কোণ): greater than 0° but less than 90°.

  • Obtuse angle (স্থূল কোণ): greater than 90° but less than 180° (e.g. 120°).

  • Straight angle (সরল কোণ): 180°, the angle on a line.

  • Reflex angle (প্রবৃক্ত কোণ): greater than 180° but less than 360° (e.g. 320°).

  • Adjacent angles (সন্নিহিত কোণ): angles with a common vertex and a common leg.

  • Complementary angles: two angles whose sum is 90° — each is the complement (পূরক কোণ) of the other.

  • Supplementary angles: two angles whose sum is 180° — each is the supplement (সম্পূরক কোণ) of the other.
