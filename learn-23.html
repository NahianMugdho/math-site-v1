২৩. Chapter 2: Quadrilaterals, Polygon

Quadrilaterals have four sides. The four interior angles sum to 360° (∠A + ∠B + ∠C + ∠D = 360°). A diagonal (e.g. AC) joining opposite vertices splits the quadrilateral into two triangles, so the area of ABCD = area ΔABC + area ΔACD. The perimeter is the sum of the four sides.

Parallelogram: a quadrilateral is a parallelogram if (i) its opposite sides are equal, or (ii) its opposite sides are parallel, or (iii) its opposite sides are both equal and parallel.

  • AB = CD, AB ∥ CD, AD = BC, AD ∥ BC, ∠A = ∠C, ∠B = ∠D.

  • ΔAED ≅ ΔBCE, ΔAEB ≅ ΔDEC; the areas of ΔABE, ΔBCE, ΔCDE and ΔADE are equal. Diagonals AC and BD bisect each other at E.

  • Perimeter = 2(a + b). Area = base × height = BC × h.

Rectangle: a parallelogram whose angles are 90° or whose diagonals are equal. A = lw, P = 2(l + w). N.B. every rectangle is a parallelogram, but not every parallelogram is a rectangle.

Rhombus: like a parallelogram, but all four sides are equal. Its diagonals bisect each other at right angles.

  • P = 4a. A = a × h = ½ d₁ × d₂.

  • N.B. d₁ and d₂ are not necessarily equal. Every rhombus is a parallelogram but not every parallelogram is a rhombus. A rhombus's angles are not necessarily 90°.

Square: a rhombus whose angles are 90° (or diagonals equal); equivalently, a rectangle with equal sides (or perpendicular diagonals).

  • A = x² = ½ d². P = 4x.

  • N.B. every square is a rhombus, a rectangle and a parallelogram, but the reverse is not necessarily true.

  • x² = ½ d² means the area of a square is half the area of the square drawn on its diagonal.

Trapezoid: two sides are parallel and the other two are non-parallel. A = ½ (h₁ + h₂) × h, where h₁ and h₂ are the parallel sides and h is the height.

Polygon: a triangle is the smallest polygon; shapes with more sides are also polygons. For a polygon with n sides, total interior angle = (n − 2) × 180°.

Sides Name Total interior angle = (n − 2)180°
3 Triangle 180°
4 Quadrilateral 360°
5 Pentagon 540°
6 Hexagon 720°
7 Heptagon 900°
8 Octagon 1080°
9 Nonagon 1260°
10 Decagon 1440°

  • Regular polygon: all sides (and angles) equal. Each angle = (n − 2)180° / n. Example: regular decagon, each angle = 1440°/10 = 144°.

  • Hexagon: 6 sides, sum of interior angles = 720°; each angle of a regular hexagon = 120°.

  • A hexagon has 9 diagonals. In a regular hexagon of side x, the longest diagonal (L.D.) = 2x, and there are three L.D.s. A regular hexagon divides into 6 equilateral triangles.

  • Area of a regular hexagon = 6 × (√3/4) x² = (3√3/2) x².
