২৫. Chapter 4: Solid Geometry

Rectangular solid (length l, width w, height h)

  • It has 8 vertices and 12 edges. Total edge length = 4(l + w + h).

  • Total surface area (TSA) = 2(lw + lh + wh).

  • Volume = base × height = l × w × h.

  • The three face diagonals are √(l² + w²), √(l² + h²) and √(w² + h²). The longest diagonal (between opposite vertices) = √(l² + w² + h²).

  • N.B. volume is the amount of space an object occupies, i.e. how much it can hold.

Cube — like a rectangular solid, but all edges are equal (l = w = h). For edge x:

  • Total edge length = 12x

  • TSA = 6x²

  • Volume = x³

  • Short (face) diagonal = x√2

  • Longest diagonal = x√3

Sphere of radius r: Volume = (4/3)πr³. TSA = 4πr².

Cylinder with height h and circular base radius r:

  • TSA = 2πrh + 2πr² = 2πr(h + r)

  • Volume = base × height = πr²h

Cone with OB = radius r, AO = height h and AB = slant height l:

  • Volume = (1/3)πr²h

  • TSA = πr(r + l)

N.B. the unit of a solid's volume is cubic inches, cubic feet, cubic metres or c.c. (cubic centimetres).
