Set A: IBA-style Practice (Arithmetic Focus)

Self-timed mock set with worked solutions

  1. A can finish a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish it?

  2. A tap fills a tank in 6 hours; a leak empties the full tank in 9 hours. If both work together, how long to fill the empty tank?

  3. Alloy P has copper and zinc in the ratio 3 : 2. Alloy Q has them in the ratio 1 : 4. Equal weights of P and Q are melted together. What is the copper : zinc ratio in the new alloy?

  4. 20 litres of a 30% acid solution is diluted with water to make a 20% solution. How many litres of water are added?

  5. Tk 20,000 is invested at 10% per year compound interest, compounded annually. What is the amount after 2 years?

  6. A sum becomes double in 8 years at simple interest. What is the yearly rate of interest?

  7. An article is sold at 20% profit for Tk 1,800. (a) What is its cost price? (b) At what percent loss would it be sold for Tk 1,350?

  8. A shopkeeper gives two successive discounts of 20% and 10%. What single discount is equal to this?

  9. The average of 5 numbers is 24. When one number is removed, the average of the remaining four is 22. What number was removed?

  10. A father is 4 times as old as his son. After 6 years he will be 3 times as old. Find the present age of the father.

  11. A car goes from A to B at 40 km/hr and returns at 60 km/hr. What is its average speed for the whole trip?

  12. A boat's speed in still water is 12 km/hr and the current is 3 km/hr. How long will the boat take to go 45 km downstream and return the same distance upstream?

  13. The price of an item is raised by 25% and then reduced by 20%. What is the net percent change?

  14. How many integers satisfy both 3x − 5 < 10 and x ≥ −2?

  15. (a) How many different arrangements are there of the letters of the word BANANA? (b) A committee of 3 men and 2 women is to be formed from 5 men and 4 women. How many committees are possible?

  16. In a class of 60 students, 35 like Mathematics, 30 like English and 10 like neither. How many like both?
