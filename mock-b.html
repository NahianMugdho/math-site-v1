Quantitative Comparison

For each pair choose: (A) Quantity A is greater; (B) Quantity B is greater; (C) the two quantities are equal; (D) the relationship cannot be determined.

  1. x > 0. Quantity A: x². Quantity B: x.

  2. Quantity A: 20% of 50. Quantity B: 50% of 20.

  3. 0 < x < 1. Quantity A: x³. Quantity B: x².

  4. For the data set {3, 5, 7, 9, 26}. Quantity A: the mean. Quantity B: the median.

  5. Quantity A: circumference of a circle with radius 4. Quantity B: perimeter of a square with side 6. (Use π ≈ 3.14.)

Multiple Choice and Numeric Entry

  1. The ratio of boys to girls in a class of 40 is 3 : 5. How many girls are in the class?

  2. If 3x + 2y = 16 and x − y = 2, what is x?

  3. Two fair dice are rolled. What is the probability that the sum is 7?

  4. Numeric entry: What is 15% of 240?

  5. Select all that apply: Which of the following are prime? 51, 53, 57, 59, 61

  6. Numeric entry: What is the sum of the interior angles, in degrees, of a hexagon?

  7. A right circular cylinder has radius 3 and height 10. What is its volume (in terms of π)?

  8. What is the distance between the points (−1, 2) and (5, 10)?

  9. In how many ways can 3 people be chosen from a group of 8?

  10. If 2x+1 = 32, what is x?

  11. If a + b = 10 and ab = 21, what is a² + b²?

Data Interpretation

Quarterly sales of a company (in thousands of Tk): Q1 = 120, Q2 = 150, Q3 = 135, Q4 = 180.

  1. What is the percent increase from Q1 to Q2?

  2. What is the percent increase from Q3 to Q4 (to the nearest whole percent)?

  3. What is the average quarterly sales?
