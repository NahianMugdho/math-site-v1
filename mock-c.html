Mathematical Reasoning

  1. Find the HCF and LCM of 12 and 18.

  2. What is the smallest number exactly divisible by 4, 6 and 10?

  3. Evaluate 0.3 + 0.03 + 0.003.

  4. If a − b = 3 and ab = 10 (a, b positive), find a + b.

  5. Find √0.0081.

  6. If 25% of x equals 30% of 50, find x.

  7. Tk 1,000 is divided among A, B and C in the ratio 2 : 3 : 5. How much does each get?

  8. What is the average of the first 10 natural numbers?

  9. What is the simple interest on Tk 5,000 at 8% per year for 3 years?

  10. What is the area of an equilateral triangle of side 4 cm?

  11. What is the diagonal of a square of side 5 cm?

  12. What is the sum of the interior angles of an octagon?

  13. The circumference of a circle is 44 cm. Find its radius and area (take π = 22/7).

  14. What is the angle between the hands of a clock at 4:30?

  15. A cube has edge 3 cm. Find its volume and total surface area.

Mental Ability

  1. Find the next number: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?

  2. Find the next number: 3, 6, 11, 18, 27, ?

  3. In a certain code, CAT is written as 24 (sum of letter positions). How is DOG written?

  4. A man walks 5 km north, then 3 km east, then 5 km south. How far is he from the start, and in which direction?

  5. Pointing to a man, Rina says, "He is the son of my father's only sister." How is the man related to Rina?

  6. Odd one out: 8, 27, 64, 100, 125.

  7. The ages of two friends are in the ratio 3 : 4 and their sum is 35 years. Find their ages.
