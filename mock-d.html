Set D: Bank Exam-style Practice (Bangladesh Bank AD, Sonali, Janata, Agrani, etc.)

Self-timed mock set with worked solutions

  1. Find the simple interest and total amount on Tk 8,000 at 7.5% per year for 4 years.

  2. Find the compound interest on Tk 10,000 at 5% per year for 2 years (annual compounding).

  3. A trader buys 40 articles for Tk 1,000 and sells all of them for Tk 1,200. Find the profit percent.

  4. The marked price of a shirt is Tk 2,000. A 15% discount is given, then 5% VAT is added on the discounted price. What is the final price?

  5. A loan of Tk 12,000 carries 10% simple interest for 2 years and is repaid in 24 equal monthly instalments. What is each instalment?

  6. Tk 54,000 is divided among three people in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find each share.

  7. 6 men can complete a work in 15 days. In how many days can 10 men complete it?

  8. Pipe A fills a tank in 10 hours and pipe B in 15 hours. Both are opened together for 2 hours, then A is closed. How long in total until the tank is full?

  9. The average salary of 20 employees is Tk 30,000. When the manager's salary is included, the average rises by Tk 1,000. What is the manager's salary?

  10. A town's population is 20,000 and grows 10% each year. What will it be after 2 years?

  11. A 150 m long train runs at 54 km/hr. How long to pass a pole, and how long to cross a 250 m platform?

  12. A man rows 6 km/hr in still water. The river flows at 2 km/hr. How long will he take to row 16 km upstream?

  13. Of 100 bank customers, 60 have savings accounts, 45 have current accounts and 20 have both. How many have neither?

  14. If x + 1/x = 3, find x² + 1/x².

  15. A rectangle is 12 cm by 5 cm. Find its perimeter, area and diagonal.

  16. A card is drawn from a standard 52-card pack. What is the probability it is a heart or a king?

  17. Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0.

  18. The sum of three consecutive odd numbers is 63. Find them.
