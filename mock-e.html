Set E: Mixed Challenge (Harder, Cross-Exam)

Self-timed mock set with worked solutions

  1. A and B together finish a job in 6 days, B and C in 8 days, and A and C in 12 days. How many days will all three take together?

  2. A mixture of milk and water has 40% water. How much water must be added to 30 L of this mixture to make water 50%?

  3. A sum of Tk 15,000 amounts to Tk 19,965 in 3 years at compound interest compounded annually. What is the yearly rate?

  4. A mother is 4 times as old as her daughter. In 10 years she will be 2 times as old. Find their present ages.

  5. A train covers the first half of a journey at 30 km/hr and the second half at 60 km/hr. What is its average speed?

  6. In a regular polygon each interior angle is 150°. How many sides does it have, and how many diagonals?

  7. A cone has base radius 6 and height 8. Find its slant height, volume and total surface area (in terms of π).

  8. How many 3-digit numbers can be formed from the digits 1–5 with no digit repeated, and how many of them are even?
