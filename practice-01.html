⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • Divisible by 3 or 9: digit sum divisible by 3 or 9. By 4: last two digits. By 8: last three digits. By 7: double the last digit and subtract it from the rest; repeat.

  • Number of trailing zeros in n! = n/5 + n/25 + n/125 + … (whole parts only).

  • Equal-remainder trick: the largest number that divides a and b leaving the same remainder r is HCF(a − r, b − r). Number leaving remainder r with several divisors: LCM + r.

  • For "smallest n-digit number divisible by k": divide the smallest n-digit number by k, round up, multiply back.

  • To test a prime, divide only by primes up to the square root.
