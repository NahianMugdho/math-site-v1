⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • BODMAS: brackets, orders, division/multiplication, addition/subtraction.

  • Recurring decimals: 0.aaa… = a/9; 0.abab… = ab/99; 0.a(bb…) = (ab − a)/90.

  • Memorise fraction → percent: 1/2 = 50, 1/3 = 33.3, 1/4 = 25, 1/5 = 20, 1/6 = 16.7, 1/8 = 12.5, 1/9 = 11.1, 1/10 = 10.

  • Compare fractions by cross-multiplication. Dividing by 5 = multiply by 2 and move the point; by 25 = multiply by 4 and move 2 places.

  • Telescoping: 1/(n(n+1)) = 1/n − 1/(n+1). (1 − 1/2)(1 − 1/3)…(1 − 1/n) = 1/n.

  • Multiply by 99 or 101: n × 100 − n, or n × 100 + n.
