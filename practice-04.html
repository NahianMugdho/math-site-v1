⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • Average = sum/count, so sum = average × count. Use sums for every "removed / added / replaced" problem.

  • Change in average × number of items = change in total.

  • Average of an arithmetic progression = (first + last)/2 = middle term.

  • Deviation method: pick a guess average, add the signed differences, divide by n, adjust.

  • Weighted average = Σ(weight × value)/Σ weights. Equal distances → harmonic mean 2xy/(x+y).

  • Overlapping group averages: sum of both groups minus the total gives the shared element.
