গ্রুপ ৫: Ages

20 timed problems tagged by exam style

IBABCSBankGRE
⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • The difference between two ages never changes. Use it to link "then" and "now".

  • Let the younger (or smaller) person's present age be x; express the others in x and form one equation.

  • After n years each person adds n; the sum of k people's ages adds k × n.

  • Ratio form: if ages are in ratio a : b now, write them as ak and bk; use one time-shifted statement to find k.

  • Always check that ages come out as whole, realistic numbers.

  1. A father is 30 years older than his son. In 5 years he will be 3 times the son's age. Find the son's present age. (BCS)

  2. Ages of two people are in the ratio 3 : 5 and their sum is 56. Find the ages. (Bank)

  3. The sum of the present ages of A and B is 55. Five years ago A was twice as old as B. Find A's present age. (IBA)

  4. A man is 3 times as old as his son. After 15 years he will be twice as old. Find the man's present age. (GRE)

  5. The average age of 5 family members is 24 and the youngest is 4. Find the average age of the others. (Bank)

  6. The average age of 4 persons is 30. A fifth person aged 20 joins. Find the new average. (BCS)

  7. Rina is 4 years older than Sima and the sum of their ages is 28. Find Rina's age. (Bank)

  8. The ratio of the ages of a father and son is 7 : 2. After 12 years it will be 5 : 2. Find their present ages. (IBA)

  9. A is 5 years older than B and B is twice as old as C. If the sum of their ages is 40, find A's age. (GRE)

  10. Eight years ago a mother was 4 times her daughter's age. Now the sum of their ages is 56. Find the daughter's present age. (BCS)

  11. Two brothers differ by 6 years. In 4 years the elder will be 1.5 times the younger. Find their present ages. (Bank)

  12. The sum of a father's and son's ages is 40. Ten years later the father will be twice the son's age. Find their ages. (IBA)

  13. The ages of two people are in the ratio 4 : 5 and differ by 6 years. Find the ages. (BCS)

  14. Three siblings have consecutive ages that sum to 36. Find the eldest's age. (GRE)

  15. The ages of two people are in the ratio 5 : 3. After 4 years the sum of their ages is 40. Find their present ages. (Bank)

  16. A man is 24 years older than his son. In 2 years the man will be twice as old as his son. Find the man's age. (IBA)

  17. Five years ago the sum of the ages of a father and son was 40. Find the sum now. (BCS)

  18. Anna is half as old as Ben. In 10 years Anna will be 2/3 of Ben's age. Find Ben's present age. (GRE)

  19. The average age of three sisters is 15 and their ages are in the ratio 2 : 3 : 4. Find the eldest's age. (Bank)

  20. A father's age is 3 more than 4 times his son's age. The sum of their ages is 63. Find the father's age. (BCS)
