⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • x% of y = y% of x. Pick whichever is easier (8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2).

  • Percent change = change / original × 100. Always divide by the original.

  • Successive changes a% then b%: net = a + b + ab/100 (use negative values for decreases). Up 20% then down 20% is net −4%.

  • Price up r%: to keep spending the same, cut consumption by r/(100 + r) × 100%. Price down r%: increase consumption by r/(100 − r) × 100%.

  • Side up x% → area up 2x + x²/100 %. Radius down 50% → area down 75%.

  • Use "assume 100": pick 100 as the starting value for any percent-only problem.
