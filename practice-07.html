গ্রুপ ৭: Profit, Loss and Discount

⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • Profit and loss are always on cost price; discount is always on marked price.

  • SP = CP × (100 + p)/100 for profit and CP × (100 − l)/100 for loss. Reverse it to find CP.

  • Marked price → discount → SP: multiply the factors. Mark up 40% then discount 25% → 1.4 × 0.75 = 1.05 → 5% profit.

  • "Cost of x items = selling price of y items": profit % = (x − y)/y × 100.

  • Two articles sold at the same price, one at p% gain and the other at p% loss → net loss of p²/100 %.

  • Dishonest weights: profit % = (true weight − false weight)/false weight × 100.

  • Chain of dealers: multiply the profit factors.
