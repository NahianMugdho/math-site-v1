গ্রুপ ৮: Simple and Compound Interest

⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • Simple interest I = P × N × R/100. Amount = P + I.

  • A sum becomes k times itself at SI in (k − 1) × 100/R years. Doubling time = 100/R.

  • Compound interest: A = P(1 + r/100)ⁿ. CI = A − P.

  • CI − SI for 2 years = P(r/100)². For 3 years = P(r/100)²(3 + r/100).

  • Half-yearly compounding: use rate/2 and time × 2. Quarterly: rate/4 and time × 4.

  • Rule of 72 (compound): doubling time ≈ 72/r years.

  • Splitting an investment: set up one equation for total interest.
