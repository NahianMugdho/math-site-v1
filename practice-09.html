⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • a : b = c : d ⇒ ad = bc. Fourth proportional to a, b, c = bc/a. Third proportional to a, b = b²/a. Mean proportional = √(ab).

  • To combine a : b and b : c, make the common term equal (LCM of the b-values).

  • Dividing a total in ratio a : b : c: each part = total × (its term / sum of terms).

  • Alligation: Cheaper : Dearer = (Dearer − Mean) : (Mean − Cheaper). Water has price 0.

  • Replacement: after n replacements of x out of V, original left = V(1 − x/V)ⁿ.

  • Partnership: profit ratio = (investment × time) ratio.

  • Direct proportion: cross-multiply. Inverse: multiply the pairs (men × days = constant).
