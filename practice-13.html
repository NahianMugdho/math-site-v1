⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • Order matters → permutation nPr. Order does not matter → combination nCr. nCr = nC(n − r). nC2 counts handshakes and pairs.

  • Arrangements with repeats: n!/(p! q! r!). Circular arrangements: (n − 1)!.

  • Digits problems: fill the restricted position first (for even numbers, the last digit), then the rest.

  • "At least one" → 1 − P(none). Independent events multiply. Without replacement, update the totals.

  • Diagonals of an n-gon = nC2 − n. Triangles from n points with no 3 collinear = nC3.

  • Probability = favourable / total. All probabilities lie between 0 and 1.
