⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • Complementary angles sum to 90°; supplementary to 180°. Angles around a point sum to 360°.

  • Triangle: angles sum 180°; exterior angle = sum of the two remote interior angles; the longest side is opposite the largest angle.

  • Right triangles: Pythagoras a² + b² = c². Learn 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25. The 30°-60°-90° sides are in the ratio 1 : √3 : 2. The 45°-45°-90° sides are 1 : 1 : √2.

  • Areas: triangle ½bh; equilateral (√3/4)a²; Heron √(s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)); parallelogram bh; rhombus ½d₁d₂; trapezoid ½(a + b)h.

  • Polygon: angle sum (n − 2) × 180°; each regular interior angle (n − 2) × 180°/n; each exterior angle 360°/n; diagonals n(n − 3)/2.

  • Similar triangles: sides in ratio k → areas in ratio k².

  • The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right angles; a rhombus's side = √((d₁/2)² + (d₂/2)²).
