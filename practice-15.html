⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • Circle: C = 2πr; A = πr²; use π = 22/7 when r is a multiple of 7. Sector area = (θ/360) × πr²; arc length = (θ/360) × 2πr.

  • An angle in a semicircle is 90°. The inscribed angle is half the central angle on the same arc. The perpendicular from the centre bisects a chord, so r² = (chord/2)² + d².

  • Tangent length from a point at distance d from the centre = √(d² − r²).

  • Solids: cuboid V = lwh, TSA = 2(lw + lh + wh), diagonal √(l² + w² + h²). Cube V = a³, TSA = 6a², diagonal a√3. Cylinder V = πr²h, TSA = 2πr(h + r). Cone V = ⅓πr²h, TSA = πr(r + l), l² = r² + h². Sphere V = 4⁄3 πr³, TSA = 4πr².

  • Coordinates: distance √((x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²); midpoint = average of coordinates; slope = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁).

  • Triangle area from coordinates: half the base times the height when a side lies on an axis.
