⚡ Shortcuts and techniques

  • Series: check differences, second differences, ratios, squares or cubes (n² + 1, n² − 1), alternating patterns, primes and Fibonacci.

  • Clock angle at H hours M minutes = absolute value of (30H − 5.5M); if it exceeds 180°, subtract from 360°. The hands coincide about every 65 5⁄11 minutes.

  • Calendar: an ordinary year has 1 odd day, a leap year has 2. Find days ÷ 7 and count the remainder forward from the known weekday.

  • Direction problems: draw a quick sketch with north up; use Pythagoras for the final distance.

  • Coding: write letter positions (A = 1 … Z = 26); look for shifts or sums.

  • Blood relations: draw a family tree; read statements from the speaker outward.

  • Ranking: total = rank from top + rank from bottom − 1.
